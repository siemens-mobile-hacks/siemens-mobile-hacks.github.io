# Siemens Flash Reader by Andromeda

**Author:** Andromeda<br/>
**Platforms:** x10/x25 (HIGOLD)

Siemens Flash Reader reads All Flash, Language Area, EEPROM, Program, RAM, and
User Area of Siemens C25, S25, C35, and S35 phones via a service cable. The data
can be viewed and edited by address, and then saved as BIN or HEX.
