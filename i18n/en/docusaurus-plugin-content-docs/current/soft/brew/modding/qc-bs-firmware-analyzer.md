# QC BS Firmware Analyzer

**Author:** Viper BJK<br/>
**Platforms:** BREW

![QC BS Firmware Analyzer: screenshot 1](./qc-bs-firmware-analyzer/img/qc-bs-firmware-analyzer.png)

QC BS Firmware Analyzer is intended for analyzing BenQ-Siemens firmware on the
Qualcomm platform and working with the phone through the diagnostic port.

Features:

- Parsing AMSS and the firmware partition table;
- Extracting certificates and service blocks;
- Verifying signatures and checksums of firmware files;
- Calculating and fixing the CRC30 of the QCSBL header;
- Encrypting and decrypting service data;
- Calculating Mastercode, NetLockCode, and other keys;
- Working with the bootloader and Download Mode;
- Executing bootloader functions and flashing the modem.

The program was distributed as Open Source. The archives preserve ready-made builds,
the project source code, and the MIRACL library used by the author.
